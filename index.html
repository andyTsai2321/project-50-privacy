50計畫 · Project 50

隱私政策

這個 app 的設計前提是：你的紀錄放在你的手機上。除非你自己開啟雲端同步，否則它不會離開這台裝置。

50計畫（Project 50）由位於台灣的個人開發者 Ho Tsai 開發與維運。 本政策中的「我們」指的就是他，他也是你個人資料的控制者。

生效日 2026-08-29 · 版本 1.0

一、簡短版

不開同步：你的挑戰、習慣、打卡與筆記只存在手機裡，我們看不到，也沒有伺服器存著它。

開了同步：這些內容會加密傳輸到我們位於日本的資料庫，只有你登入後才讀得到。

另外還有：一組匿名的使用統計，用來知道有多少人走到付費那一步。它不含你的帳號、也不含任何你寫下的內容。

我們不做：不販售你的資料、不提供給廣告商、沒有廣告識別碼（IDFA）、不跨 app 追蹤你。

二、留在你手機裡的資料

預設情況下，以下全部只存在本機資料庫，不會上傳。

  • 挑戰設定：名稱、天數、起始日、連續中斷時怎麼算
  • 你的習慣：名稱、目標、單位、順序
  • 每日打卡紀錄與每日筆記
  • 提醒時間、解鎖狀態、以及已用掉免費額度的日期

把 app 從手機刪除，這些資料就一起消失。如果你沒有開過同步，那就沒有任何副本存在別的地方——請自行留意。

三、雲端同步（選用）

只有在你解鎖並登入之後才會啟用。未登入的使用者不會有任何一列資料進到我們的資料庫。

會上傳什麼

就是上一節列的挑戰、習慣、打卡與每日筆記，加上一個代表「這是你」的帳號識別碼。

帳號怎麼來的

你可以用 Apple 或 Google 登入。我們從中取得的是電子郵件地址與一組帳號識別碼。我們拿不到、也不曾經手你的密碼——驗證全程由 Apple 或 Google 完成。

使用 Apple 登入時，你可以選擇「隱藏我的電子郵件」，我們就只會看到 Apple 產生的轉寄地址。

存在哪裡

項目說明
服務商Supabase（資料庫與帳號驗證）
存放地點日本
傳輸全程 HTTPS 加密
存取控制每一列資料都綁定你的帳號，其他使用者讀不到

你的資料會離開台灣。這是使用這項服務的必要條件，開啟同步即表示你同意這件事。

四、匿名使用統計

用來回答一個問題：有多少人走到付費那一步。沒有它，我們只能用猜的。

app 會在四個時間點各送出一筆事件：完成初始設定、每次打卡、看到解鎖畫面、完成購買。每筆包含：

  • 一組隨機產生的裝置識別碼——由 app 自己產生，不是 IDFA，不會跨 app 使用，刪除 app 即永久消失
  • 事件名稱與時間
  • 打卡事件另含兩個數字：已用掉幾格免費額度、目前是挑戰第幾天

這裡面沒有你的帳號、電子郵件、習慣名稱，也沒有任何一個字的筆記內容。這份資料只寫得進去、讀不出來——app 沒有讀取它的權限。

因為它完全匿名、無從對應到特定個人，所以刪除帳號時不會一併刪除這些事件。

由於不涉及廣告識別碼、也不跨 app 追蹤，本 app 不需要、也不會顯示 iOS 的追蹤許可（ATT）詢問。

五、提醒通知

每日提醒是由你的手機自己排程的本機通知。我們沒有推播伺服器，也不會取得或上傳任何推播權杖。你把提醒設在幾點，這件事我們並不知道。

六、購買

解鎖是一次性的 App 內購買，付款全程由 Apple 處理。我們不會收到、也無法查看你的付款方式、信用卡號或帳單資訊。

app 只會向 App Store 詢問「這個 Apple ID 是否買過這個項目」，並把答案存在本機。

七、App 更新

app 會向 Expo（EAS Update）檢查是否有新版本。這個過程中，Expo 會取得你的 IP 位址與裝置／版本等技術資訊，這是所有網路請求都無法避免的。更新檢查不會夾帶你的任何個人資料或打卡內容。

八、我們合作的第三方

App Store 審查指南 5.1.1(i) 要求我們明確說明這件事。

除了前面各節提到的對象，我們不把你的資料交給任何人。這些對象是：

  • Supabase——存放你開啟同步後的資料，以及帳號驗證
  • Apple／Google——你選擇的登入方式；付款則一律由 Apple 處理
  • Expo——app 更新檢查

我們與這些服務商之間有資料處理協議，要求他們對你的資料提供與本政策相同或同等的保護， 並且只能為了提供上述服務而處理這些資料。我們沒有母公司、子公司或關係企業能存取你的資料。

九、我們不做的事

  • 不販售、不出租、不交換你的個人資料
  • 不提供給廣告商或資料仲介
  • 不使用廣告識別碼，不做跨 app 或跨網站追蹤
  • 不接第三方分析 SDK
  • 不讀取你的通訊錄、相簿、位置或健康資料——app 根本不會要求這些權限

唯一的例外是法律要求：若我們收到具有法律效力的正式要求，可能必須揭露相關資料。

十、安全與事故通知

資料在傳輸過程中全程加密，雲端上的每一列都綁定你的帳號，其他使用者讀不到。我們只保留提供服務所必要的資料——這本身就是最有效的保護：沒有蒐集的東西，就不會外洩。

如果發生可能危害你權益的資料外洩事件，我們會在知悉後儘速透過你的電子郵件或 app 內通知你，說明影響範圍以及我們採取的措施。

十一、保存與刪除

你可以隨時刪掉全部

在 app 中前往 設定 → 刪除帳號與雲端資料。這會立即且永久地刪除雲端上你的所有挑戰、習慣、打卡、每日筆記，以及帳號本身。這個動作無法復原，我們沒有備份可以還原給你。

保存期間

只要你的帳號存在，同步的資料就會一直保留——這正是同步的用途。你刪除帳號，資料就跟著消失。

撤回同意

你隨時可以在設定中登出，停止資料再往雲端傳送；要連同已上傳的資料一起清除，就用上面的刪除帳號。 兩者都不影響你留在手機上的紀錄。

本機資料

把 app 從手機刪除即可清除。這個動作不會影響雲端資料，若你也要刪除雲端資料，請先在 app 內執行上面的刪除帳號。

十二、你的權利

依照台灣《個人資料保護法》，你可以要求查詢、閱覽、複製、補充、更正你的個人資料，或請求停止蒐集、處理、利用及刪除。

實務上最快的方式是直接在 app 裡操作：你的資料本來就完整顯示在畫面上，刪除也只需要一個按鈕。若需要以其他形式提供，請透過下方聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

如果你認為我們處理你的個人資料的方式不當，除了直接與我們聯繫，你也有權向主管機關提出申訴——在台灣是本服務所屬行業的中央目的事業主管機關，在歐洲經濟區則是你所在國家的資料保護主管機關。

十三、兒童

本 app 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

十四、政策變更

本政策如有修改，會更新頁面頂端的生效日期。若涉及重大變更，我們會在 app 內另行告知。

Privacy Policy

This app is built on one premise: your records live on your phone. Unless you turn on cloud sync yourself, they never leave the device.

50計畫 (Project 50) is developed and operated by Ho Tsai, an independent developer based in Taiwan. "We" throughout this policy means him; he is the controller of your personal data.

Effective 2026-08-29 · Version 1.0

1. The short version

Sync off: your challenges, habits, check-ins and notes exist only on your phone. We cannot see them, and no server holds a copy.

Sync on: that content is transmitted, encrypted, to our database in Japan. Only you, signed in, can read it.

Also: a set of anonymous usage events, so we can tell how many people reach the paywall. They contain neither your account nor anything you wrote.

What we don't do: we don't sell your data, don't hand it to advertisers, don't use an advertising identifier (IDFA), and don't track you across apps.

2. Data that stays on your phone

By default, all of the following lives only in the local database and is never uploaded.

  • Challenge settings: name, length, start date, how a broken streak is counted
  • Your habits: name, target, unit, order
  • Daily check-ins and daily notes
  • Reminder time, unlock status, and which dates used up your free allowance

Delete the app and this data goes with it. If you never turned sync on, no copy exists anywhere else — please keep that in mind.

3. Cloud sync (optional)

Active only after you unlock and sign in. Not a single row from a signed-out user reaches our database.

What gets uploaded

Exactly what is listed in the previous section — challenges, habits, check-ins and daily notes — plus an identifier that says "this is you".

Where the account comes from

You can sign in with Apple or Google. What we receive is your email address and an account identifier. We never receive or handle your password; authentication happens entirely with Apple or Google.

With Sign in with Apple you may choose "Hide My Email", in which case we only ever see the relay address Apple generates.

Your data leaves Taiwan. That is inherent to using this service; turning on sync means you accept it.

4. Anonymous usage events

They answer one question: how many people reach the point of paying. Without them we would be guessing.

The app sends one event at four moments: finishing setup, each check-in, seeing the unlock screen, and completing a purchase. Each contains:

  • A randomly generated device identifier — created by the app itself, not the IDFA, never used across apps, and gone for good when you delete the app
  • The event name and a timestamp
  • Check-in events also carry two numbers: how much of the free allowance is used, and which day of the challenge it is

There is no account, no email address, no habit name, and not one word of your notes in any of this. The data can only be written, never read back — the app has no permission to read it.

Because these events are fully anonymous and cannot be tied to any individual, deleting your account does not delete them.

Since no advertising identifier is involved and there is no cross-app tracking, this app does not need — and will never show — the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Reminders

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your own phone. We run no push server and never obtain or upload a push token. The time you set your reminder to is not something we know.

6. Purchases

Unlocking is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never receive and cannot see your payment method, card number, or billing details.

The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID has bought this item, and stores the answer locally.

7. App updates

The app checks Expo (EAS Update) for new versions. In doing so, Expo receives your IP address and technical details such as device and version — unavoidable for any network request. Update checks carry none of your personal data or check-in content.

8. The third parties we work with

App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i) requires us to state this explicitly.

Beyond the parties named in the sections above, we give your data to no one. Those parties are:

  • Supabase — stores your data once you turn on sync, and handles authentication
  • Apple / Google — whichever sign-in you choose; payments are handled by Apple alone
  • Expo — app update checks

We have data processing agreements with these providers that require them to provide the same or equal protection of your data as stated in this policy, and to process it only in order to provide the services above. We have no parent, subsidiary, or affiliated entity with access to your data.

9. What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal data
  • We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers
  • We use no advertising identifier and do no cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • We embed no third-party analytics SDK
  • We don't read your contacts, photos, location, or health data — the app never requests those permissions

The one exception is the law: if we receive a legally valid demand, we may be required to disclose relevant data.

10. Security and breach notification

Data is encrypted in transit, and every row in the cloud is bound to your account so that no other user can read it. We keep only what the service needs — which is the most effective protection there is: what was never collected cannot leak.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you as soon as we become aware of it, by email or inside the app, explaining what was affected and what we are doing about it.

11. Retention and deletion

You can delete everything at any time

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account and cloud data. This immediately and permanently deletes all your challenges, habits, check-ins and daily notes from the cloud, along with the account itself. It cannot be undone; we hold no backup to restore for you.

Retention period

As long as your account exists, synced data is kept — that is the point of sync. Delete the account and the data goes with it.

Withdrawing consent

You can sign out at any time in Settings, which stops any further data going to the cloud; to also erase what was already uploaded, use the account deletion above. Neither affects the records kept on your phone.

Local data

Deleting the app from your phone clears it. That does not touch cloud data; to remove that too, run the account deletion above first.

12. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act — and, where they apply to you, equivalent rights under laws such as the GDPR — you may request access to, a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may ask us to stop collecting, processing, or using it.

In practice the fastest route is the app itself: your data is already shown to you in full on screen, and deleting it takes one button. If you need it in another form, contact us below.

If you believe we are handling your personal data improperly, in addition to contacting us you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Taiwan, the competent central authority for this sector; in the EEA, the data protection authority of your country.

13. Children

This app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of the page is updated. For material changes we will also say so inside the app.
